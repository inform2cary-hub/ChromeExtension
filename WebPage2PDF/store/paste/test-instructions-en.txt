No credentials needed. Open any web page, click the toolbar button, then "Convert to PDF". Chrome shows its own debugging notice while the export runs; it disappears when the PDF is saved.

To verify the debugger claims: the only chrome.debugger calls are in src/background/service-worker.js (attach, detach, sendCommand), and the only CDP methods passed to sendCommand are "Page.enable", "Page.printToPDF" and "Emulation.setEmulatedMedia". The package contains no fetch, XHR or WebSocket call.
